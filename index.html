How It Works

From Call to Cleanup
in Four Steps

Our streamlined process ensures the fastest possible response when disaster strikes.

1

Emergency Call

Call our 24/7 hotline. A live dispatcher assesses your situation and begins mobilization immediately.

2

Rapid Assessment

Our team arrives on-site within hours to evaluate damage, document conditions, and create a recovery plan.

3

Execute Recovery

Crews deploy with heavy equipment and materials. Tarping, board-up, debris removal, and water extraction begin.

4

Final Walkthrough

Complete documentation provided for insurance. Final inspection ensures your property is secured and protected.
