Our Capabilities

Emergency Roof Tarping

When storm damage exposes your roof, every minute counts. Our emergency tarping crews deploy with commercial-grade, UV-resistant tarps and professional fastening systems. We don't just throw a tarp on your roof — we engineer a water-tight seal that will hold through continued severe weather.

We use 2 methods for our tarping — Non-Invasive (Sandbags) & Invasive (Naildown) procedures.

✓ Non-Invasive method: sandbag-secured tarps
✓ Invasive method: professional naildown installation
✓ Commercial-grade UV-resistant materials
✓ Engineered fastening systems
✓ Sub-4-hour response time
✓ Insurance-ready documentation
🌪️

Storm Damage Cleanup

Complete debris removal and site restoration after hurricanes, tornadoes, severe storms, and flooding events. Our trained crews handle hazardous materials, structural debris, and environmental cleanup with full regulatory compliance.

✓ Heavy equipment fleet on standby
✓ Hazardous material certified
✓ Residential & commercial scale
✓ Environmentally responsible disposal
🏠

Board-Up & Securing

Protect damaged properties from further weather damage, unauthorized entry, and vandalism. Our board-up services secure windows, doors, and structural openings using reinforced materials and professional installation techniques.

✓ Windows, doors & structural openings
✓ Reinforced plywood & polycarbonate options
✓ Security deterrent systems
✓ Temporary fencing available
💧

Water Extraction & Drying

Industrial-grade water extraction, structural drying, and moisture mitigation. We deploy high-capacity pumps, dehumidifiers, and air movers to remove standing water and prevent mold growth and long-term structural damage.

✓ Industrial pumps & extractors
✓ Commercial dehumidification
✓ Moisture mapping & monitoring
✓ Mold prevention treatment
