The People Behind ADS

Meet the Team That
Runs Toward the Storm

Experienced professionals who've dedicated their careers to protecting communities when disaster strikes.

Josh Wilson

CEO / Co-Founder

Josh brings over 20 years of hands-on experience in the restoration and disaster response industry, having personally overseen recovery operations following some of the most destructive storms in recent memory, including Hurricane Ian, Hurricane Idalia, and multiple major tornado outbreaks across the Southeast and Gulf Coast. Trained in OSHA safety standards and experienced in large-loss project management, he has built and scaled multiple companies before co-founding ADS with the goal of transforming how America approaches disaster recovery. As CEO, Josh sets the strategic vision for the company, cultivates relationships with insurance carriers, and ensures every deployment reflects ADS's commitment to speed, transparency, and quality. He is driven by a belief that disaster-affected communities deserve better — faster mobilization, clearer communication, and a more compassionate recovery process that puts people first.

josh@americandisastersolutions.com

✉ ☎

Kyle Sewell

COO & Co-Founder

With more than 15 years of experience mobilizing and managing field teams for large-scale disaster response, Kyle has directed crews through hurricane landfalls, widespread tornado damage, severe hailstorm events, and catastrophic flooding across Texas and the broader Gulf region. He is trained in OSHA 30-Hour construction safety protocols and experienced in IICRC water damage restoration standards, bringing a disciplined, safety-first approach to every job site. As COO, Kyle oversees all field operations — from rapid crew deployment and equipment logistics to on-site quality control and real-time progress reporting back to insurance partners. His systems-driven approach to storm response consistently reduces cycle times and claim severity, delivering measurable results for carriers and faster relief for policyholders. Kyle is motivated by the impact the work has on families and communities, and he takes pride in building a field operation that performs at the highest level even under the most demanding conditions.

kyle@americandisastersolutions.com

Zane Mason

President

Zane provides executive leadership and strategic direction for American Disaster Solutions, drawing on years of experience in disaster recovery operations and business management. He has been instrumental in scaling the company's capacity to handle simultaneous large-loss deployments, including coordinating multi-state response efforts during back-to-back hurricane seasons and severe weather outbreaks. Trained in project management and experienced in restoration industry best practices, Zane oversees company-wide operations, vendor relationships, and growth initiatives that expand ADS's reach into new markets. As President, he ensures that every crew, every job, and every client interaction upholds the standards that set ADS apart in the industry. Zane's approach is rooted in accountability and continuous improvement — he believes the disaster response industry can and should deliver a better experience for everyone involved, from the adjuster to the homeowner.

zane@americandisastersolutions.com

Amber Sewell

Documentation & Claims

Amber brings extensive experience in insurance documentation and claims coordination to her role at American Disaster Solutions, where she oversees the accurate and timely recording of all field work, damage assessments, and project milestones. She has managed documentation for hundreds of disaster recovery claims stemming from hurricanes, tornadoes, hail events, and flood damage across multiple states. Experienced in Xactimate estimating software and well-versed in carrier-specific documentation requirements, Amber ensures that every claim package is complete, compliant, and submitted promptly to minimize delays for policyholders. She works closely with adjusters and field teams alike to keep communication flowing and paperwork on track, even during high-volume catastrophe events. Amber is driven by the knowledge that thorough, well-organized documentation directly translates to faster settlements and quicker recovery for the families ADS serves.

amber@americandisastersolutions.com

Bonnie Roberts

Documentation & Claims

Bonnie is known for her meticulous attention to detail and deep understanding of insurance industry requirements, skills she has honed through years of administrative and documentation work in the disaster recovery space. She has supported claims processing for large-scale storm events including Hurricane Ian and multiple severe weather seasons across the Gulf Coast and Southeast, ensuring that every file meets carrier standards before submission. In her role at ADS, Bonnie coordinates documentation workflows, tracks claim statuses, and serves as a key point of contact between the office and field teams to ensure nothing falls through the cracks. She is experienced in insurance compliance protocols and skilled at identifying potential issues before they become delays, saving time for adjusters and policyholders alike. Bonnie's commitment to getting the details right reflects her belief that the administrative side of disaster recovery is just as important as the work happening on the roof.
